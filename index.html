Activity status and milestone risk

Forecast logic: Bar position and length represent each activity's full dated calendar window on a common May 6-November 15 scale; one-day activities are shown as milestone markers. Each bar is filled to its actual % complete — using the earned measure where one exists (trackers 93.3%, inverter stations 50.0%, LV/DC 21.4%) and the completed-unit measure elsewhere (piles 97.7%, modules 86.8%), so no activity reads 0% while physical work stands on site, and the dark tick inside the bar marks the plan-to-date % for today's date (linear from the activity's start position to 100% at its finish). Fill short of the tick means the activity is behind its own plan; the point variance is printed under each activity name and in the table. Activities with pending or dependency-based dates remain in the table but are excluded from the bar chart. Activities may overlap by released block, but project-level finishes must follow the physical installation sequence.
