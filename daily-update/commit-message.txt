Electrical by line: re-base the ledger onto the real lines and onto Juan de la Chica's Sep 8 plan

The three tables in the #electrical section were static HTML frozen at the Aug 24
basis. They were still publishing L1 homerun 33,523 / trunk 4,786 / harness 134
against a ledger of 57,886 / 17,394 / 896, and the daily log stopped on Aug 19.
They are now rendered by renderElectricalByLine() from data.electricalByLine and
cannot go stale again.

105.9% of its homerun take-off, 199% of its trunk and 194% of its boxes.
